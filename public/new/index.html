02 · Immediate help

First: stop making it worse.

Water

Shut off the source

If you can do it safely. Main shutoff where the line enters the house; an appliance has its own valve behind it.

Fire / smoke

Do not wipe soot

Wiping sets it into paint and grout. Soot lifts chemically, not with a rag. Keep the HVAC off.

Electrical

Stay out of standing water near power

Do not enter water near energized equipment, outlets or the panel. Kill the breaker only if you can reach it dry.

Document

Wide photos before anything moves

Before moving or removing anything. Timestamps are evidence — for you, for your adjuster, for us.
